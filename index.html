Methodology & Disclaimers

  1. Data sources. AI / Joule lead stream, Sales Closure Repository, Digital app downloads sheet, Ground Leads Repository + legacy Ground sheet (deduplicated by phone, earliest touch), Other_all_data (Adhoc / Rescue / DNP Pool / IVR / Refer & Earn / Interakt campaign), and Meta Ads Manager export.
  2. Phone-number quality. The AI Joule export stores phones in scientific notation (e.g. 9.18595E+11), destroying precision for ~31% of rows. We bridge through the older AI_Lead_Flow.csv via (Name, Lead Date) for the Jan 2025 – May 2026 overlap. Re-export Joule as text to make the join exact.
  3. Tata vs Non-Tata — Leads. Classified only via the SUBSCRIPTION_PLAN_INTEREST column. If it contains "success", "experience", or "tata.ev" (case-insensitive substring), the lead is Tata. Everything else (including blanks and "Could not be ascertained") is Non-Tata.
  4. Tata vs Non-Tata — Sales. Classified purely by subscription plan name. Plans containing "success" or "experience" → Tata. All others → Non-Tata.
  5. Tata vs Non-Tata — Ad Spend. Tagged by Meta campaign name containing "tata". This is intentionally different from the lead/sale rule above — ads come from Meta which only has the campaign label, not the interest column.
  6. Campaign Segments. Resolved in priority order from the Meta campaign name: (1) contains "tata" → Tata; (2) is a Post / Instagram post → SM Post; (3) "Campaign 4–8" → Focused; (4) "Campaign 1–3" → Standard; otherwise Other. Tata wins ties (e.g. "Campaign 5 Tata" is Tata, not Focused).
  7. Sales scope. Excludes Renewal, B2B Fleet, and any sale where the Subscription Sold starts with "B2B". Result: 442 sales since Jan 2025 (down from 469 — removed 27 B2B-named plans).
  8. Attribution paths. For each sale we look up the customer's phone across AI / App / Ground / Other tables and stitch together every touch dated strictly before the sale-closure date, in chronological order. Consecutive identical sources are collapsed. First touch source is just the first leg of that path. The First Touch Source filter at the top affects every section.
  9. Location of sales. All sales are treated as Delhi-NCR. The "Outside Delhi-NCR" and "Unknown" location filters therefore show 0 sales.
  10. KPI formulas. AI Qualify Rate = Qualified ÷ Total leads · P1 Leads shown with % of total · Repetitive Leads = MCS − New Messaging Contacts, with % of MCS · Delta = AI Leads − Meta MCS (= AI conversations beyond Meta).
  11. Ad metrics by filter. Spend / Reach / Clicks / MCS / New Messaging Contacts respond to Campaign Segment and Date filters only — Meta does not attribute them at the geography or AI-quality level. Leads & sales respond to all filters.
